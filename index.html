One thing runs late, and the whole day slides.

Most students don't need a stricter schedule. They need help when the schedule stops matching the day. Here's a normal Monday for Maya, a junior with 15 credits, a campus job, and a club to run.

  1. 9:30

    Lecture runs ten minutes over. She grabs coffee on the way out.

  2. 11:20

    Starts the problem set 20 minutes late, so it eats into lunch.

  3. 2:00

    Café shift. This one can't move.

  4. 6:00

    Skips the gym to catch up on the problem set.

  5. 10:45

    The essay draft never happened. Tomorrow already looks full.

What TimeWise does about it

  • See today at a glance

    What's happening now, what's next, and how much free time you actually have before the day ends. Overlaps and overloaded days get flagged before they become a problem.

    Open Today →

  • Fix the rest of the day when you fall behind

    Say how late you're running. Classes and shifts stay where they are, everything else shifts, and whatever won't fit moves to tomorrow.

    Try replanning →

  • Look back without the guilt trip

    Mark what happened to each block. You'll see one pattern (like an overrun that knocked out the evening) and one small change for tomorrow.

    Look back on a day →

  • Works with the calendar you already have

    Read-only connections to Google Calendar and Notion, or upload a calendar file from Apple Calendar, Outlook, or Moodle.

    Bring in a calendar →
