Why TimeWise

Most planning apps assume the plan is the hard part. For college students it usually isn't. The hard part is 11 AM on a Monday, when one thing ran late and the rest of the day no longer fits.

What TimeWise is built on

Plans fall apart. That's normal.
Lectures run over, shifts get extended, a friend needs help. TimeWise assumes this will happen and is built around recovering, not around keeping a perfect streak.
Fixing the day matters more than planning the week.
When you're behind, you need to know what to drop and what to move, in under a minute. That's what Replan does: classes and shifts stay put, everything else shifts, and what won't fit moves to tomorrow.
No guilt.
A block that didn't happen gets an offer to put it back, not a red X. Looking back on a day shows one pattern and one small change, not a score.
Use the calendar you already have.
Students already live in Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Notion, or Moodle. TimeWise reads from them instead of asking you to start over.

A day with TimeWise

Maya is a junior with 15 credits, a campus job, a club officer role, and internship applications. Here's how TimeWise fits into one of her days.

  1. 8:15 AM

    Checks Today

    She sees what's next, that she has about 3½ free hours before 11 PM, and a heads-up that Wednesday is packed.

  2. 11:20 AM

    Falls behind

    Lecture ran long and she's 30 minutes behind. She taps I'm running behind. Her shift stays, the gym moves, and the essay draft goes to tomorrow.

  3. 9:40 PM

    Looks back

    She marks what happened. TimeWise points out that one long block knocked out the rest of her evening and suggests a 45-minute buffer after Tuesday's lab.

Where it stands

TimeWise is a student project for BUS 131A. Here's what works today and what's still an idea.

Works in the demo

  • Today view with overlaps, packed days, and missed blocks
  • Your week from typing, Google Calendar, Notion, or a calendar file
  • Replanning the rest of a day
  • Looking back on a day
  • Saving your week when you sign in with Google
  • A 2-minute practice session

Not built yet

  • A phone app with reminders
  • Suggestions for when to work on an assignment
  • Recurring events you set up inside TimeWise
  • Syncing changes back to your calendar
