# TimeWise brand voice

Status: **Draft pending owner approval**, except where marked Observed (already in [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and on the live site).

## Core rules

| Rule | Status |
|---|---|
| Plans fall apart, and that's normal. Write for recovering, not for keeping a perfect streak. | Observed (DESIGN.md principle 1) |
| No guilt: missed blocks get an offer to put them back, never a red X or a score. | Observed (DESIGN.md principle 4) |
| Write like a friend who's good at planning: short, specific, no hype, no decorative emoji. | Observed (DESIGN.md principle 5) |
| Start from a real student moment (a lecture that ran over, a shift at 2), not a general claim about college. | Draft |
| Say exactly what happens when someone clicks: "Your shift stays, the gym moves to 5." | Draft |
| Sentences you could say out loud. One idea per sentence. | Draft |
| Name the limits honestly: "read-only", "not built yet", "Google will warn you it's unverified." | Draft (consistent with about.html "Where it stands") |
| Respect the student's choice. Suggest; don't instruct or pressure. | Draft |
| Never invent testimonials, numbers, or founder stories. | Draft |

## Examples

| Use | Avoid | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Running behind? Tell TimeWise what changed." | "Never fall behind again!" | Promise TimeWise can't keep |
| "Missed something? That's fine. Put it back in one tap." | "You missed 2 tasks today ❌" | Guilt and a score |
| "Bring the calendar you already use." | "Revolutionize your scheduling workflow." | Buzzwords |
| "What would make TimeWise more useful for you?" | "Rate your amazing experience!" | Leading question |
| "Google will warn the app isn't verified. Click Advanced, then Go to TimeWise." | (saying nothing) | Unexplained friction erodes trust |

## Visual notes (Observed, styles.css section 1)

- Coral `#e8684a` only for now/next and one key moment per screen. Teal `#1f6e72` for buttons.
- Headings Schibsted Grotesk; body Atkinson Hyperlegible Next; times IBM Plex Mono. The 2026-10-27 video used Inter as an owner-approved stand-in.
- Hairlines and space instead of boxes; shadows only on floating things.
- Do not rely on color alone: overlaps also say "Overlaps", packed days say "packed".

## Audit checklist for any new copy

- [ ] Starts with a situation or a result, not a slogan
- [ ] Every feature mentioned exists today (check about.html "Not built yet")
- [ ] No stress/productivity/grade promises
- [ ] No guilt words (failed, missed out, behind schedule as a judgment)
- [ ] No decorative emoji
- [ ] Captions and text readable on a phone
- [ ] One clear next step
