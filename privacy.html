Privacy at TimeWise

Last updated: September 30, 2026

TimeWise is a student project concept being tested with real users. This page explains, in plain language, what the site collects, why, and how to remove it.

You can use TimeWise without an account

Everything on the site works without signing in. Anything you do on Try It while signed out stays in your browser tab and disappears when you leave the page.

If you sign in with Google

TimeWise asks Google only for your name, email address, and a Google account ID. It does not get access to your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or calendar through sign-in. We use this only to create your account, show your name in the header, and link your saved week and feedback to you.

While you're signed in, TimeWise saves:

  • Your week on Try It — the events you add or import, replans you apply, and your Plan vs. Reality check-ins — so it's there when you come back. If you import events from a calendar while signed in, their titles and times are saved as part of your week.
  • Feedback you send — what it's about, which part of the site, an optional rating, and your message.

A secure cookie (tw_session) keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. It can't be read by the site's scripts, and signing out deletes it.

Connecting a calendar

Google Calendar and Notion connections are read-only and only look at the current week. Google Calendar events go straight from Google to your browser. Notion items pass through this site's server on their way to you and aren't stored there; your Notion connection is remembered with a secure cookie (tw_notion) for up to 7 days. Uploaded .ics files are read in your browser and never uploaded.

Early access and anonymous usage counts

The early-access form saves your answers and, if you give it, your email, which is only used to tell you when TimeWise launches. To learn which parts of the demo are useful, the site also counts, without names, which features a visitor used at least once, using a random ID stored in your browser.

Who can see your information

Only the TimeWise project team (site admins). Admins can see testers' names and emails, sign-up dates, and feedback. They can't see your password (there isn't one — Google handles sign-in) or your session. We don't sell your information, show ads, or share it with anyone else. Data is stored with Cloudflare, which hosts the site.

TimeWise's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your information

Signed in? Go to My account → Delete my account. That permanently deletes your account and saved weeks and signs you out. Feedback you've sent is kept to help improve the site, but it's no longer linked to you. To remove anything else, such as an early-access email, send a note with the 💬 Feedback button or contact the project team.
