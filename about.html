The Idea

Less guilt, more realistic plans.

College students don't need another to-do list. They need practice turning a messy week into a plan that fits — and a kind nudge when it falls apart. Here's the thinking behind TimeWise.

The Solution

Practice time management like a skill — because it is one.

TimeWise turns reflection into a short, supportive practice session instead of a lecture about productivity. No shame, no overwhelm — just small, honest check-ins that build real habits.

  • ✓ Start by naming your real goals and deadlines
  • ✓ Answer one short reflection or planning question at a time
  • ✓ Get immediate, supportive feedback — every time
  • ✓ Watch your score, streak, XP, and progress grow
  • ✓ Walk away with a takeaway and a realistic next step

See It In Action

A day with TimeWise, through Maya's eyes

Meet Maya: a junior juggling 15 credits, a part-time job, a club leadership role, and recruiting season. Here's what a realistic day looks like with TimeWise as part of it.

Screen 1
🗺️

“See Everything Clearly”

The dashboard

Classes, work shifts, club meetings, social plans, deadlines, exams, and recruiting milestones all show up in one calm, color-coded view — with conflicts and available work time highlighted automatically.

Screen 2
✅

“Plan What Matters Today”

Today's plan

TimeWise suggests three realistic priorities with recommended time blocks and a short reason each one matters. Maya can accept the plan as-is, edit it, or postpone something to another day.

Screen 3
🔄

“Adjust Without Starting Over”

Replanning

When something unexpected comes up, Maya taps Replan My Day. TimeWise moves lower-priority tasks, protects real deadlines, and hands back a revised plan that still feels achievable.

Screen 4
📓

“Reflect and Keep Momentum”

End-of-day reflection

A brief journal asks what went well, what changed, and what should happen next. Progress is measured in completed meaningful actions — not just hours worked.

Who It's For

Built for students juggling a full, real life.

TimeWise is for college students balancing academics, jobs, family responsibilities, social commitments, rest, and personal goals — all at the same time. It meets you where you are, without asking you to overhaul your entire life overnight.

Why TimeWise Is Different

Not another planner. Not another tracker.

Typical planners & trackers

  • Log tasks and due dates
  • Assume you already know what to do
  • Offer little reflection or feedback
  • Can feel like one more chore

TimeWise

  • Helps you notice where your time actually goes
  • Connects that reflection to your real goals and deadlines
  • Gives immediate, supportive feedback as you practice
  • Ends every session with a practical, realistic next step

TimeWise combines self-awareness with practical support — so you don't just track your time, you understand it and act on it.
