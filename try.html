Google Calendar and Notion connections are read-only and only fetch this week. Google events go straight from Google to your browser; Notion items pass through this site's small server on their way to you, and nothing is stored there. Your Notion sign-in is kept in a secure cookie in your browser for 7 days. Everything on the grid disappears when you leave the page.
